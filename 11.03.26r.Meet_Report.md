## 1. MEETING OBJECTIVES
The primary goal was to analyze the main CAD assembly in Fusion 360, finalize the decomposition of components for manufacturing, and establish the requirements for fasteners and custom parts.

## 2. FUSION 360 ANALYSIS & ASSEMBLY STRATEGY
* **Model Decomposition:** The master assembly has been successfully separated into individual components (Internal Frame, Actuation Mechanisms, and External Shell).
* **Assembly Plan:** The team has analyzed the construction in detail and established a clear workflow for the assembly process in the coming weeks. We have a full understanding of the sequence required to integrate the internal skeleton with the outer casing.
* **Design Review:** A structural analysis was conducted to ensure that all 3D-printed parts are optimized for assembly and mechanical durability.

## 3. HARDWARE & FASTENERS SPECIFICATION
The following components have been selected for the build:

* **Screws:** Socket Head Cap Screws (Allen/Hex drive) in sizes **M2.5, M3, and M4**. 
    * We will use various lengths depending on the joint, with a **maximum length of 10mm**.
* **Washers:** Standard flat and spring washers.
* **Nuts:** Standard hex nuts.
* **Threaded Inserts:** **IMPORTANT: We will use bidirectional (two-way) threaded inserts** to ensure maximum pull-out resistance and stability within the 3D-printed parts.
* **Custom Spacers:** The team will design and 3D print custom bushings/spacers to ensure precise alignment of internal mechanisms.
