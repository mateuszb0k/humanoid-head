## 1. MEETING OBJECTIVES
The primary goals were to begin the physical assembly of the robot head, verify the functionality of all servo motors using test scripts, and finalize the setup of the PWM drivers.

## 2. ASSEMBLY PROGRESS
* **Lower Jaw & Lips:** Successfully assembled the lower jaw and bottom lip mechanisms.
* **Eyes:** Assembly of the eye mechanisms has commenced.
* **Technical Challenges:** We identified structural issues with small linkage elements in the eyes and lips. Several 3D-printed parts proved to be too brittle and prone to cracking during assembly.

## 3. ELECTRONICS & FIRMWARE TESTING
* **Servo Verification:** * All **19 MG90S** servos were tested. One unit was found to be defective and was replaced with a spare from private resources.
    * Both **MG996R** servos were confirmed to be fully functional.
* **Driver Integration:** * Both **PCA9685 PWM drivers** were tested and are operational. 
    * The team gained hands-on experience with the drivers' API and successfully established control over the servo channels using custom test code.

## 4. NEXT STEPS
* **Design Iteration:** Redesign the brittle linkage components for the eyes and lips to improve mechanical durability.
* **Manufacturing:** Generate and prepare new print files (G-code/STL) for the reinforced parts.
* **Eyes Completion:** Finalize the assembly of the eye mechanisms once the new parts are printed.
* **Integration:** Begin planning the mounting of the PCA9685 drivers and the power distribution for the MG996R servos.
