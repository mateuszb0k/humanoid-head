## 1. MEETING OBJECTIVES
The primary goal was to define the software-to-hardware mapping, establish safety protocols for the servomechanisms, and plan the development of emotional expressions.

## 2. SOFTWARE & SERVO CONFIGURATION
* **Servo Addressing:** We initiated the process of mapping physical servos to their digital addresses in the control system.
* **Safety Limits:** The team discussed the necessity of establishing individual hardware limits (min/max PWM angles) for each servo to prevent mechanical binding and over-extension.
* **Movement Code:** Initial development of the servo control algorithms has begun, focusing on smooth transitions and coordinated movements.
* **Expression Logic:** Preliminary discussions took place regarding the implementation of "emotions" – mapping multiple servo movements to specific emotional states.

## 3. ELECTRICAL & MECHANICAL REFINEMENTS
* **Wiring Standards:** A plan was established to finalize the electrical schematic following the completion of servo addressing. 
* **Reliability:** We decided to rework the wire terminations using a crimping tool (crimper) to ensure professional and secure electrical connections.
* **Fastener Security:** To address the recurring issue of loosening screws in the lips and eyebrows, we evaluated two solutions:
    * Application of thread-locking fluid (Loctite/Threadlocker).
    * Integration of nylon-insert lock nuts (Nyloc) where space allows.

## 4. NEXT STEPS
* **Stand Design:** Work has officially started on the design of the robot head stand/base.
* **Execution:** During the next meeting, we will finalize the physical addressing and hard-code the safety limits for all servos.
* **Refining Connections:** Conduct a full recrimping of all connector ends to eliminate potential intermittent signal issues.
