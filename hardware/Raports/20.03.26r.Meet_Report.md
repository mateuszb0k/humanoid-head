## 1. MEETING OBJECTIVES
The primary goals were to replace the previously broken components, complete the assembly of the jaw and lip structures, and conduct initial movement tests for the eye mechanisms.

## 2. ASSEMBLY PROGRESS & TECHNICAL ISSUES
* **Jaw & Lips:** The full structure of the lower jaw and lips has been successfully completed.
* **Eyes:** The eye mechanisms were assembled. However, some structural components failed again during the process.
* **Structural Failures:** We encountered further breakage in the lip linkages and the **nose cover**. 
* **Fastener Stability:** A potential long-term issue was identified: screws may loosen over time due to mechanical vibrations. 
    * **Proposed Solution:** Use thread-locking fluid (threadlocker) in future assembly stages.

## 3. ELECTRONICS & CONTROL TESTING
* **System Setup:** We used a **Laboratory Power Supply**, an **Arduino Uno**, and custom-written test code to drive the servos.
* **Eye Movement:** Successfully controlled and verified the range of motion for the eye servos. The integration between the software and the mechanical linkages was confirmed, despite the fragile parts.

## 4. NEXT STEPS
* **Manufacturing:** Prepare and export new print files (STL/G-code) for the broken components, specifically the eye linkages and the **nose cover**.
* **Reinforcement:** Review the CAD models for the failing parts to identify if further thickening or a change in print orientation is required before the next assembly attempt.
* **Maintenance Planning:** Procure thread-locking adhesive for the final assembly.
