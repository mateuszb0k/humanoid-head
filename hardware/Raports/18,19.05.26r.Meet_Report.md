## 1. MEETING OBJECTIVES
The focus of this multi-day sprint was to address hardware instability, integrate environmental sensors, recalibrate servo safety parameters following the driver replacement, and run extensive system integration tests.

## 2. HARDWARE MAINTENANCE & MECHANICAL REFINEMENTS
* **Servo & Linkage Replacements:** Replaced two (2) additional faulty servos and several structural linkage elements connecting the servos to the facial mechanism.
* **Camera Holder:** Upgraded and installed an improved, sturdier camera holder to enhance stability for visual tracking.
* **Mechanical Maintenance:** Applied WD-40 to critical high-friction joints to ensure smoother motion profiles and reduce strain on the actuators.
* **Enclosure Assembly:** Mounted the side panels/walls of the head stand, finalizing the structural enclosure of the base.

## 3. ELECTRONICS, SENSORS & CALIBRATION
* **PIR Sensor Integration:** Successfully installed and programmed the Passive Infrared (PIR) sensor for human motion detection.
* **Servo Recalibration:** Due to the previous PCA9685 driver replacement, we noticed a shift in the operational ranges of the servos. 
    * **Action:** The team completely reworked and re-coded the servo safety limits and facial expression angles to match the new hardware baseline.

## 4. INTEGRATION & TESTING
* **Cross-Team Integration:** Conducted extensive collaborative sessions with the software/AI teams to synthesize the hardware controls with the broader project infrastructure.
* **Stress Testing:** Ran a massive battery of movement and logic tests to verify system stability under continuous operation.
