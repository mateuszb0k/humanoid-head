# Subject: Lip Sync Implementation & Component Maintenance

## 1. MEETING OBJECTIVES
The focus was on synchronizing mechanical movement with the audio system and addressing hardware failures encountered during the integration phase.

## 2. SOFTWARE & AUDIO INTEGRATION
* **Text-to-Speech (TTS) Synchronization:** We successfully implemented a mechanical response system where the jaw moves in sync with the TTS output. This is a key step towards realistic verbal interaction.
* **Logic Calibration:** The timing between the audio stream and the servo commands was adjusted to ensure natural-looking lip-sync.

## 3. HARDWARE MAINTENANCE & REPAIRS
* **Driver Failure:** One of the **PCA9685 PWM drivers** suffered a hardware failure. 
    * **Resolution:** The faulty board was identified and replaced with a new unit. The system is now fully operational again.
* **Mechanical Refinement:** Based on ongoing tests, several minor flaws were found in the 3D models.
    * **Action:** Two specific components have been flagged for redesign and re-printing to improve fitment and durability.

## 4. NEXT STEPS
* **Mechanical Replacement:** Print and install the updated parts to replace the current ones.
* **Stress Testing:** Conduct prolonged tests of the new PCA9685 driver to ensure electrical stability under load.
* **Sensor Integration:** Move forward with the installation of the camera and PIR sensor as per the Milestone 2 plan.
