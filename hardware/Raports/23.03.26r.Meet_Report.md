## 1. MEETING OBJECTIVES
The primary goals were to complete the mechanical assembly of the head (excluding the occiput), perform synchronized servo calibration, and conduct a full-scale movement test using an external power source.

## 2. ASSEMBLY PROGRESS & CHALLENGES
* **Eyebrows & Main Assembly:** The eyebrow structure is finished, and all modules have been integrated into a single unit, except for the **occiput** (back of the head), which requires a reprint due to a previous manufacturing error.
* **Component Replacement:** Most required parts were successfully 3D printed and installed.
* **Structural Failures:** We encountered new breakages in different linkage elements within the eye mechanisms. This remains a critical area for structural optimization.

## 3. ELECTRONICS & POWER CALIBRATION
* **Power Management:** We integrated a voltage regulator (amplifier/step-down) into the circuit and calibrated it to a steady **5V output** to ensure safe operation for the electronics.
* **Servo Synchronization:** Successfully implemented mirrored and synchronized movement for the jaw servos.
* **Full-Scale Testing:** Conducted a comprehensive test of all installed servomechanisms. The system was powered by a laboratory power supply and controlled via **Arduino Uno** using a dedicated full-system test script.

## 4. NEXT STEPS
* **Occiput Reprint:** Correct the CAD/slicing errors for the occiput and start the long-format print.
* **Mechanical Refinement:** Address the recurring failures in the eye linkages; investigate if the synchronized movements are putting unexpected stress on these specific parts.
* **Final Assembly Preparation:** Once the occiput is ready, proceed with the final enclosure of the head.
